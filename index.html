FAQ

Is this really free?

Yes — it uses Kiro's own free tier and first-time upgrade offers. You pay ₹0. The $20 credit on first upgrade is an official Kiro promo shown right on their pricing page.

How many accounts can I use?

There's no fixed limit — every new account gets the free run on its own. That's exactly what makes the trick repeatable and "forever free."

Which plans do I actually get?

Over a month you ride from Pro (week 1) up through Pro+ and toward Pro Max / Power by the last week — the full paid ladder, unlocked gradually.

Do I need a credit card?

No. Sign up with social login or AWS Builder ID to start free — no card needed to begin.
